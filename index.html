AWS landing zone and account vending

Multi-account AWS with IAM boundaries, service-control policies, and budget controls. Self-service sandboxes for isolated environments, with auditability kept in the workflow. In his current role, account vending took this from a 2.5-day ticket to about five minutes across 50–100 environments.

Datadog APM, OpenTelemetry, and MLflow on one instrumentation path: session-correlated traces, token and cost metrics, replayable traces, and Terraform-managed SLOs and paging monitors. In that current role, the agreed targets were 99.5% request success and 99% tool-call success, enforced as code.

05

Cloud cost reduction

EC2 Spot strategies, systematic right-sizing, and infrastructure automation in a high-change production environment. At onPeak, that work reduced cloud infrastructure costs by about 70%.
