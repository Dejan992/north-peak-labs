Services

What a senior platform engineer is for

AI consulting for the platform itself: MLOps consulting, AI platform engineering, and LLM infrastructure, for teams in the Phoenix area and anywhere the work is remote.

01

AI/ML platform and MLOps advisory

How models get trained, evaluated, shipped, and watched. GitOps CI/CD, Python and TypeScript SDKs, and observability for the platform, including token and cost tracking, so a notebook is not the production system.

02

LLM and AI system architecture

LLM gateways, RAG data layers such as pgvector, and where a model does and does not belong. Access for AI tools with OAuth 2.1 and Okta when that is already how the company signs people in. Read the checklist for self-hosting LLMs on Kubernetes versus paying per token.

03

Cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes

AWS, including EKS and multi-account IAM and SCP guardrails, with Terraform and CDK. Kubernetes when it is the right tool, and a simpler path when it is not.

04

AI strategy

A plain recommendation for operators. What to fund, what to ignore, and what has to be true in the systems you already run before a model is worth deploying.

05

Expert-network calls

A focused call for investors, operators, and product teams who want a senior practitioner’s view of an AI platform, an MLOps setup, or LLM infrastructure before they commit.
